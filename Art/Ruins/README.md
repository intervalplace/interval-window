For the ruined half of the landmark vocabulary, which was forty-odd words all
drawn as scaled cubes: `landmark.broken-tower` (Column_*), `half-wall` and
`sunken-wall` (Floor_*, Wall pieces), `window-arch` (Arch_Gothic, Arch_Round),
`rubble-heap` (Bricks, Brick), `barricade`, and the bridge sections for
`staithe`. It also carries a second Cart, Chest, Crate, Barrel and Bookcase in
the same style as the Fantasy Props kit already in use.
