# Quaternius -- Farm Buildings Pack (Sept 2018)

Author: Quaternius (https://quaternius.com)
Pack page: https://quaternius.com/packs/farmbuildings.html
Licence: **CC0 1.0 Universal**, declared in this folder's own `License.txt`.

Fetched from the author's own Google Drive mirror.

Thirteen models: Barn, BigBarn, SmallBarn, OpenBarn, ChickenCoop, Silo,
Silo_House, WaterTower, **Windmill**, **TowerWindmill**, **Well**, Fence,
Fence2. The two windmills are what `landmark.mill` has wanted since it was a
cube, and the second Well gives a choice against the Medieval Village one.

This is the oldest of the three packs and its style is a shade simpler than
the MegaKit line; check each model beside a citizen before trusting it.
